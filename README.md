# PCM retrofits for PMAY houses: B.Tech Project, IIT Kharagpur

**Optimising phase change material (PCM) retrofits on the roof and walls of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) houses for thermal comfort and energy savings in a warm-humid climate (Kolkata / Kharagpur).**

The project simulates the West Bengal PMAY-U *Beneficiary-Led Construction* model house in EnergyPlus. It adds PCM layers to the roof and walls, scores comfort with India's residential adaptive comfort model (IMAC-R) and cooling energy with the Energy Performance Index, then searches for the best comfort / energy / cost trade-off with NSGA-II / NSGA-III.

The full problem statement is in [`BTP_Problem_Statement.pdf`](BTP_Problem_Statement.pdf).

## Key modelling choices

- **Geometry:** three thermal zones (bedroom, living + kitchen, toilet block) from the drawing's centre-line dimensions. The drawing's "up" is taken as North; orientation is tested as a sensitivity.
- **Constructions:** 250 mm burnt brick with cement plaster, a 125 mm RCC roof with 25 mm screed (slab thickness assumed), and single-glazed windows.
- **PCM:** EnergyPlus CondFD with an enthalpy–temperature curve and temperature-dependent conductivity. n-Octadecane (28 °C) is the baseline; n-heptadecane, n-nonadecane and n-eicosane are alternatives (22 / 32 / 36.6 °C).
- **Comfort:** IMAC-R, T<sub>n</sub> = 0.42·T<sub>out,30DRM</sub> + 17.6 °C, ±3.6 °C (80 %) / ±2.15 °C (90 %), over occupied hours. With ceiling fans, the upper limit is raised by 1.2 °C.
- **AC:** ideal-loads cooling with a 26 °C set-point and COP 3.3. The bedroom is cooled at night and the living room in the afternoon and evening, from 1 March to 15 November.
- **Verification:** CondFD vs CTF agree within 1 %. The grid is converged at space-discretisation constant 1 (constant 3 is used for screening). A latent-heat-off control case separates the PCM effect from the extra layer.
