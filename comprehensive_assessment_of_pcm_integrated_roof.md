# Comprehensive assessment of PCM integrated roof for passive building design: A study in energo-economics

## 1. Core Problem Being Solved

In developing nations with tropical and subtropical climates (like India), space cooling is the fastest-growing energy end-use. Roof slabs absorb massive amounts of solar radiation (up to $1\text{ kW/m}^2$), acting as the primary thermal funnel into the building. While adding PCMs into heavy Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) roofs can solve this, traditional integration methods (direct mixing or immersion) lead to chemical degradation of the concrete matrix, structural weakening, and long-term PCM leakage.

## 2. State of the Art & Progress Made

**State of the Art:** Previous studies explored micro-encapsulation or embedding PCMs in lightweight wallboards, but these lack the high-capacity thermal mass required for heavy concrete roofs facing severe solar loads.
**Progress Made:** This research introduces a novel structural integration: embedding macro-encapsulated PCMs inside **metal void formers** bidirectionally within the concrete matrix.

* **Total Leak Prevention:** The metal shell securely isolates the PCM from the highly alkaline concrete.

* **Thermal Performance:** The experimental slab (Exp-SU) reduced indoor peak temperatures by $4.0^\circ\text{C}$, cut heat flux into the room by **60.6%**, and reduced total thermal loading by **49.8%**.

* **Economic Viability:** The system yields a daily electricity saving of up to $0.04\text{ USD/kWh/m}^2$, translating to a **simple payback period of 5.7 years**.

* **Carbon Reduction:** Limits $CO_2$ emissions by up to $4.8\text{ kgCO}_2\text{/kWh}$ depending on the grid's fuel source.

## 3. Tools, Techniques, and Formulas Used

* **Material:** **OM37**, a commercial non-paraffin organic PCM with a high transition temperature of $37^\circ\text{C}$, matched to Rupnagar's extreme summer climate. DSC confirmed a latent heat capacity of $173.91\text{ kJ/kg}$.

* **Void Formers:** Cold-forged from Al 1100 aluminum alloy (thermal conductivity $222\text{ W/mK}$) into a hybrid shell-and-cylinder shape to maximize structural strength and internal convection.

* **Unit Cell Testing:** Cast $300\text{ mm} \times 300\text{ mm} \times 150\text{ mm}$ RCC unit cells. Conducted outdoor, heavily insulated 1D downward heat transfer tests monitored by J-type thermocouples.

* **Fourier's Law for 1D Heat Transfer:**
  

  $$
  Q = K_{eff} A (T_{ext} - T_{int})
  $$

* **Effective Thermal Conductivity (**$K_{eff}$**):**
  

  $$
  \frac{1}{K_{eff}} = \frac{1}{h_{ext}} + 2\frac{\delta_{cc}}{k_{cc}} + 2\frac{\delta_{Al}}{k_{Al}} + \frac{\delta_{Air}}{k_{Air}} + \frac{\delta_{PCM}}{k_{PCM}} + \frac{1}{h_{int}}
  $$

* **Thermal Load Integration (**$q_d$**):**
  

  $$
  q_d = \int q dt
  $$

* **Electricity Cost Savings (**$E_{cs}$**):**
  

  $$
  E_{cs} = HGR_{max} \times U_{PE} \times 24\text{ hr}
  $$

* **Carbon Emission Savings (**$C_s$**):**
  

  $$
  C_s = E_f \times E_{cs}
  $$
